# Samsa v0.6.0: exhaustive model checking, per-topic retention, key compaction, and storage fault repair

Production plan milestone v0.6 (`docs/PRODUCTION.md`, items 6, 11, 12a, 12b,
and 13; plus items 19, 23a, 24, and packaging landed on `main`).

## What is new

- **Exhaustive checking of small configurations** (B2, item 11, ADR 0025):
  breadth-first state-space exploration of three Raft nodes (`TestExhaustive`
  to depth 10 in `go test` and depth 12 in CI, across classic and lease/PreVote
  modes, with power loss and message drop bounds), plus shortest-counterexample
  search (`TestExhaustiveShortest` finds `AckBeforeSync` in 11 events). Found
  and removed an unsound one-node partial-order reduction (`docs/BUGS.md` M11).
  Report: `docs/reports/b2-exhaustive.md`.
- **Per-topic retention and copy-forward cleaner** (C2 part a, item 12a,
  ADR 0026, ADR 0027, format level 2): per-topic `retention.ms` and
  `retention.bytes` via `CreateTopics` and `IncrementalAlterConfigs` (API 44,
  forwarded from followers); head-segment copy-forward cleaner (`cmdRelocate`)
  so short-retention topics do not stay pinned behind long-retention ones in
  the shared Raft log. Report: `docs/reports/c2-retention-part-a.md`.
- **Key compaction** (C2 part b, item 12b, ADR 0029, format level 3):
  `cleanup.policy=compact` (and `compact,delete`), `delete.retention.ms`, and
  `min.compaction.lag.ms`; leader soft key-digest map and `cmdCompact` batch
  rewrites preserving offsets across gaps; simulator invariants C1–C5 and five
  compaction mutations caught. Report: `docs/reports/c2-retention-part-b.md`.
- **Storage faults, background scrub, and peer repair** (B1, item 13, ADR 0030):
  background scrub of sealed log segments, durable `damaged` and `recovering`
  markers, truncation/reset of corrupt suffixes or snapshot-backed prefixes, and
  repair from the cluster's remaining nodes while abstaining from elections and
  votes until recovered (invariant S7, three mutations caught). Report:
  `docs/reports/b1-storage-faults.md`.
- **24-hour soak passed** (G5, item 6): `termIndex` (`internal/raft/terms.go`)
  keeps historical terms as runs below `logStart - 1`; 24 h run produced and
  consumed 43,196,136 records (41.2 GiB) with 0 errors, 0 gaps, 32 MiB peak RSS
  (25 MiB at 24 h), and 63 ms restart time. Report: `docs/reports/g5-soak.md`.
- **Also included on `main`:** fetch from the nearest replica (KIP-392, item 19,
  ADR 0031, `docs/reports/c19-follower-fetch.md`), four additional application
  fault kinds (`expire`, `emptyfetch`, `slowfetch`, `outofrange`, item 23a,
  `docs/reports/a23a-fault-kinds.md`), configuration profiles and `samsa doctor`
  (item 24, `docs/reports/p24-profiles.md`), container image (`make image`),
  systemd unit, runbook (`docs/guides/runbook.md`), and security review
  (`docs/reports/g8-security-review.md`).

## Upgrading from earlier releases

Rolling restart one node at a time with SIGTERM (`TestRollingUpgrade`). Format
levels 2 (per-topic retention configs and `cmdRelocate`) and 3 (key compaction
and `cmdCompact`) are raised automatically once every voter runs v0.6.0 or
later. Downgrading below a raised format level is not supported.
